FOR YOUR EVENT

What we bring.

You bring the players together. We take care of MatchBoard, setup and on-site support.

What's included

  • MatchBoard with Player Buttons and a display for one court.
  • Event configuration, setup and removal.
  • Personal on-site support throughout your booked period.
  • Event or sponsor branding to suit the equipment.

Optional extras

  • Video recordings of selected matches.
  • Selected highlights as personal clips.
  • Video feedback for your camp.
  • Longer support, additional courts or further equipment.

We agree the available courts, equipment and extras with you in advance. For video options, the selection, clip count and delivery date are set out in your quote. Your team arranges court hire, coaches, catering and event or tournament organisation, including the draw, schedule and rankings.
