GETTING STARTED AS A CLUB

Bring ÉliteReplay
to your court.

From the first demonstration to your own court: together, we'll plan MatchBoard around your venue and playing group.

01 / YOUR COURT

Show us where you play.

Your club, location, courts and existing equipment. Plus your plans: regular matches, club nights, training or events.

02 / YOUR EQUIPMENT

Decide together.

Display and Player Buttons for MatchBoard; cameras as well for replays. We establish what's already available, what to add and how it fits together on court.

03 / YOUR FIRST MATCHES

Personal support.

We install the agreed setup on site and support your first matches. You get to know the process right on your own court.

What you provide.

A point of contact, access to the chosen court and a group of players to get started together.

What we agree beforehand.

What you already have, what your court still needs, and where the display and cameras will go. We agree on power, connectivity and recording conditions before starting.

Made to fit your club's day-to-day.

Who keeps score during the match?

Players enter points using one Player Button per team. Press once to add a point, twice to undo it. You don't need an extra member of staff to keep score.

What does our court need?

For MatchBoard, we plan a display and Player Buttons; for replays, cameras too. On site, we agree on placement, power and connectivity. We factor in the equipment you already have.

Who handles setup and support?

ÉliteReplay personally supports installation, configuration and your first steps. Your club provides a point of contact and court access. We agree on the start-up process, who to contact with questions and ongoing support before use.

For camps, events and tournaments, we support the agreed court on site throughout the booked period. More about the mobile service.

Equipment, process and support are agreed before you start. Capture remains a standalone app.
