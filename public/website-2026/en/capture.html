ALSO WITH TWO DEVICES

Two angles. One shared score.

Pair two iPhones, or an iPhone and an iPad, using a QR code. Each team confirms its points with a hand gesture, and both devices show the same score. Recordings stay on their respective devices.

We'll try the right camera positions and two-device mode with you in the supported pilot.

CAPTURE / YOUR CONTROLS

Stay in the game.
You give the signal.

Choose the controls that suit your coaching or match. Capture responds to your signal: save a replay, count a point or control a recording.

FLIC 2

One button. Right by your side.

Trainer Tool: Save a replay without walking over to the iPhone.

Player Mode: one click for Team A, double-click for Team B, press and hold to undo.

APPLE WATCH

Everything on your wrist.

Trainer Tool: Trigger a replay, start and stop a commentary or your own demonstration.

Player Mode: Start a match, add points for A or B and undo the last point.

HAND GESTURE · BETA

An open hand is all it takes.

Trainer Tool: trigger a replay with an open hand.

Player Mode: confirm the warm-up and add points for your assigned team. With two devices, each team scores on its own side.

You confirm the point yourself. The gesture does not automatically detect rallies or point winners. Choose the control that fits your mode; replay buttons remain available in the coaching app.

YOUR DEVICE / YOUR START

What you need for Capture.

An iPhone or iPad running iOS/iPadOS 16 or later, positioned securely with a view of the action. Flic 2 and Apple Watch are optional controls; the Watch requires watchOS 26.2 or later. Hand gestures use the front camera. Keep Capture open and the device unlocked while recording.

Which camera do I need for slow motion?

Slow-motion recording requires a rear camera that can record Full HD (1080p) at 120 frames per second. Standard recording works with the front or rear camera. Before you start, we'll check which recording options your iPhone model supports.
